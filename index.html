Our Legacy

History of BMS SR. SEC. SCHOOL

BMS SR. SEC. SCHOOL, Bass (Hisar) is a reputed Senior Secondary School committed to providing quality education with strong moral values, discipline and character building. Since its establishment, the school has continuously worked towards developing confident, responsible and knowledgeable students who are prepared for future challenges.

The school offers a healthy learning environment where academics, sports, cultural activities and personality development are given equal importance. Our experienced teachers focus on individual attention, innovative teaching methods and practical learning so that every child can achieve academic excellence.
