Activities Overview

Morning Assembly

English Newspaper Reading by students. Students are selected according to their Roll Number from different classes.

Weekly Quiz

General Knowledge Quiz is conducted every Saturday. Teachers also ask random educational questions inside classrooms.

Art Competition

Regular Drawing and Art Competitions. Winners receive prizes during Morning Assembly in front of all students.

Sports

Special Sports Activities are organized on scheduled activity days. Students participate in various indoor and outdoor games.

Student Recognition

Winners are called on the stage during Morning Assembly. Certificates and prizes are distributed to encourage excellence.
