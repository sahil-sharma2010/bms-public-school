Examination System

A structured and transparent assessment system designed to evaluate every student's academic progress and overall development.

Examination Time

  • Examinations generally begin at 09:00 AM or 10:00 AM according to the school schedule.
  • All examinations conclude at 12:00 PM.

Examination Environment

  • Every class is allotted a separate examination room.
  • Two teachers are assigned to supervise each examination room.
  • Students are expected to maintain strict discipline throughout the examination.
Assessment Cycle

Major Examinations

📅 Half-Yearly Examination

  • The Half-Yearly Examination is conducted every year in September.
  • It evaluates the syllabus covered during the first academic session.

📚 Final Examination

  • The Final Examination is conducted after completion of the full syllabus.
  • Students are rigorously assessed on all prescribed subjects.

Board Class Preparation
(Class X & XII)

🎓 Special Academic Support

  • Special offline classes are conducted during the Summer Holidays.
  • These classes usually continue for approximately 10 to 15 days.
  • After completion of every chapter, chapter-wise tests are conducted.
  • Regular practice tests continue throughout the academic session.
  • Approximately 2–3 months before the Board Examination, intensive subject-wise revision tests and model papers are conducted.
  • Special academic attention is given to Class X and Class XII students to ensure excellent board examination performance.

Non-Board Classes

📖 Academic Assessment

  • For all non-board classes, the Half-Yearly Examination is conducted in September.
  • After syllabus completion, regular class tests are conducted to strengthen preparation.
  • Teachers frequently ask oral and written questions during classroom teaching to assess understanding.

Student Performance Monitoring

Performance Review

Teachers continuously monitor every student's academic performance.

Special Guidance

Students requiring additional support receive special guidance.

Parent Communication

If a student's academic performance is consistently below expectations, parents are informed and appropriate guidance is provided to improve learning outcomes.
