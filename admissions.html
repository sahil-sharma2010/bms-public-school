Admission Process

1

Step 1

Collect the Admission Form from the School Office or download it if available.

2

Step 2

Fill in all required student and parent details carefully.

3

Step 3

Attach all required documents.

  • Birth Certificate
  • Previous School Report Card
  • Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhaar Card Copy
  • Passport Size Photographs
4

Step 4

Submit the completed application form along with the required documents at the school office.

5

Step 5

The school will verify all submitted documents.

6

Step 6

After successful verification, complete the admission fee payment.

7

Step 7

Receive the admission confirmation and welcome kit from the school.
